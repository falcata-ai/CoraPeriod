This policy explains how Cora: Journal & Calendar handles information. For privacy, support or deletion requests, contact faturatakiphizmet@gmail.com.

1. Personal records on your device

Cora stores the information you enter on your device: optional profile details and preferences, period dates, symptoms, mood, journal notes, water amounts in millilitres, a personal water goal, optional weight, legacy questionnaire answers and session history. These inputs support personal record keeping and calendar estimates. General movement and rest sessions are chosen by the user by duration and activity, not prescribed from symptoms or health goals. Journal text is not used to diagnose pregnancy or measure hormone levels. Core personal records do not require an email account.

Cora does not automatically upload these health records for purchase verification or attach them to feedback. It does not include an advertising or behavioural analytics SDK, and does not sell health records for advertising.

2. Purchases through Google Play

Google Play processes Android payments and subscriptions. Cora uses Google Play Billing on your device to query purchases for the signed-in Play account and checks the purchase signature using this appâ€™s public licensing key. Product identifiers, signed purchase information and purchase tokens are processed on the device and by Google Play. Cora does not send them to a Cora subscription verification server. Payment card details are handled by Google, not entered into Cora. Journal and health records are not included in billing requests.

Cora stores a local access status and last-check time. Offline PRO access is cached for up to 24 hours after a successful Play check; this is not the subscriptionâ€™s renewal or expiry date. Reconnect to Google Play to refresh access. Google Play shows the actual subscription terms, renewal and cancellation information. Restoring purchases uses the Play account on the device; it does not transfer your journal or health records.

3. Feedback you choose to send

The feedback form prepares an email in your chosen email app. You review and send it yourself; opening a draft does not send it. The category, subject, message, selected sentiment, language and optional contact email you include are processed by your email provider and the support inbox. Your health history is not attached. Avoid adding sensitive information you do not want to share. Support emails are kept in the receiving mailbox until deleted; contact faturatakiphizmet@gmail.com for deletion requests.

4. Optional sharing and exported files

JSON backups and PDF exports are created when you request them. You choose where to save or share them. Anyone you share a file with can keep a copy. Cora does not automatically upload your backups. JSON backups include saved water amounts, the water goal and optional weight. A weight-based goal is only an approximate planning aid, not a measurement of your medical fluid needs. App lock restricts app access but does not encrypt exported files.

This local version does not provide server-based partner sharing. If you choose to share an exported file, your selected app or provider handles delivery.

5. Notifications, widgets and permissions

If you enable phone motion, the device motion sensor animates the water illustration. Sensor readings are processed temporarily on the device; they are not saved, included in backups or sent to a server. You can turn this feature off. Sensor listening stops while the app is in the background or the water view is not visible.

Notification permission is optional and is used for local water, journal, calendar and plan reminders. A home-screen widget may show a summary of saved information. Consider whether others can see your screen. Cora does not request contacts, microphone or location access for these features. Notification visibility and permission can be controlled in Android settings.

Backups, exported reports, recipient copies and support emails must be deleted separately. Deleting local records does not cancel your Google Play subscription or erase store purchase records.

Cora does not maintain a subscription or device-account database for this local version. Google manages its own transaction records under its policies. Support emails remain in the support mailbox until deleted; resetting the app does not delete them. Contact the address above for access, correction or deletion requests. You can choose what to enter, edit or delete local records, decline notifications, refrain from optional sharing and stop using the app. Depending on your location, applicable privacy rights may include access, correction and deletion. Send privacy requests to faturatakiphizmet@gmail.com. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary medical documents.

9. Health information and changes

Cora is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Period calendar dates are estimates based on entered records and must not be used for contraception. The app does not provide pregnancy tracking or clinical assessments.
