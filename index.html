Cora: Journal & Calendar
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Android package: com.cora.period

This policy explains how Cora: Journal & Calendar handles information. For privacy, support or deletion requests, contact faturatakiphizmet@gmail.com.

1. Personal records on your device

Cora stores the information you enter on your device: optional profile details and preferences, period and pregnancy dates, a clinician’s reported gestational age and assessment date, symptoms, mood, journal notes, legacy questionnaire answers and session history. These inputs support personal record keeping and calendar estimates. General movement and rest sessions are chosen by the user by duration and activity, not prescribed from symptoms or health goals. Journal text is not used to diagnose pregnancy or measure hormone levels. Core personal records do not require an email account.

Cora does not automatically upload these health records for purchase verification or attach them to feedback. It does not include an advertising or behavioural analytics SDK, and does not sell health records for advertising.

2. Purchases and service requests

Google Play processes Android payments and subscriptions. Cora receives product and purchase information. When purchase verification is configured and used, a verification service processes the product ID, platform, purchase token or transaction identifier and a service-generated device identifier to verify access, associate purchases and prevent misuse. Payment card details are handled by the store, not entered into Cora.

Online services and their hosting providers receive network information such as IP addresses when a request is made. The service uses request limits for security. Health notes are not part of purchase verification requests. Online features may be unavailable if their service is not configured.

3. Feedback you choose to send

When you submit feedback, the category, subject, message, selected sentiment, language and optional contact email are sent through the support service and email provider to the support inbox. Only form content is submitted; your health history is not attached. Avoid including health or other sensitive information you do not want to share with support. A service identifier and submission time/status support the 24-hour submission limit. Message content is sent by email rather than stored in the service database; the receiving mailbox retains the email.

4. Optional sharing and exported files

JSON backups and PDF exports are created when you request them. You choose where to save or share them. Anyone you share a file with can keep a copy. Cora does not automatically upload your backups.

If partner sharing is enabled in your version and you explicitly activate it, the selected mood, session or cycle summary is encrypted on the device and stored through the sharing service for the invited recipient. The invitation includes access material: share it only with the intended person. Revoke the connection to remove the active server-side sharing record; this does not remove copies already kept by a recipient. Versions with partner sharing disabled do not expose this feature.

5. Notifications, widgets and permissions

Notification permission is optional and is used for local reminders and subscription notices. A home-screen widget may show a summary of saved information. Consider whether others can see your screen. Cora does not request contacts, microphone or location access for these features. Notification visibility and permission can be controlled in Android settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in Profile, clear the app’s storage through Android, or uninstall the app. Clearing only the cache is different from deleting app data. Backups, exported reports, recipient copies and support emails must be deleted separately. Deleting local records does not cancel your Google Play subscription or erase store purchase records.

The verification service retains device/purchase associations to support subscription access and fraud prevention. Support emails remain in the support mailbox until removed; resetting the app does not delete them. The current service does not apply an automatic expiry to these records. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of service/support records. We may need enough information to locate your request and verify ownership. If transaction-security or legal obligations require retention, we will explain the applicable limitation. Google manages its own transaction records under its policies.

7. Security and service providers

Cora uses HTTPS for configured remote service requests. Device access controls help protect local records; no storage or transmission method is completely risk-free. Google Play, service hosting and email providers process the information necessary for the features described above and may process it outside your country. See Google’s Privacy Policy for Google services.

8. Your choices and contact

You can choose what to enter, edit or delete local records, decline notifications, refrain from optional sharing and stop using the app. Depending on your location, applicable privacy rights may include access, correction and deletion. Send privacy requests to faturatakiphizmet@gmail.com. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary medical documents.

Journal tools, app lock and widgets

Personal note tags and special-date events stay on your device and are included in JSON backups. Deleted journal entries can be restored for 30 days; expired trash is removed when the app is opened. You can permanently delete an entry from Trash earlier. Android app lock uses system biometrics or your device screen lock; Cora does not receive or store biometric templates or your device PIN. App lock restricts access to the app but does not encrypt exported backups. Home screen note previews are off by default; if you enable them, anyone viewing your home screen can read the note, even while Cora is locked. Optional event reminders are scheduled locally on Android. Notifications do not expose event titles. Permission and battery settings can affect delivery.

9. Health information and changes

Cora is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Calendar and fertility dates are estimates and must not be used for contraception. Calendar dates and gestational age are estimates based on entered dates, not clinical measurements. The app does not evaluate pregnancy test answers or provide a personalised pregnancy symptom assessment. Consult your care team for medical decisions.
